UtauTTS macOS GUI third-party addendum
=======================================

This file supplements THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt in the macOS GUI package. It
applies only to the dynamically linked Qt libraries and Qt Multimedia
deployment bundled inside UtauTTS.app.


Qt 6
====

The desktop GUI uses dynamically linked Qt 6 modules, including Qt Core,
Qt GUI, Qt QML, Qt Quick, Qt Quick Controls, Qt Multimedia, and Qt Concurrent.
The open-source Qt modules used by this application are distributed under the
GNU Lesser General Public License version 3. Users may replace the dynamically
linked Qt frameworks with compatible modified versions, subject to the
applicable license terms and ABI compatibility.

License and source information:
https://www.qt.io/licensing/
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/

The LGPLv3 text, a written source offer for the exact Qt version used by the
build, Qt replacement/relinking instructions, and Qt third-party attribution
links are included under licenses/Qt. Qt Multimedia's FFmpeg deployment and
corresponding source guidance are documented there as well.


FFmpeg libraries distributed by Qt Multimedia
==============================================

Qt Multimedia may deploy FFmpeg components. The exact files detected in the
GUI app bundle, their SHA-256 values, and the applicable source guidance are
listed in licenses/Qt/FFmpeg-SOURCE-AND-LICENSE.txt.

FFmpeg licensing depends on the exact build configuration; optional GPL
components can change the applicable terms. Refer to the exact binary's
corresponding source and the current FFmpeg legal guidance:
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
