Chinese reference model (models/english-reference/kokoro-zh)
============================================================

UtauTTS reads Chinese sentences with Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh, compares the speech
with the voicebank recordings through WavLM features, and synthesizes the
output from the voicebank frames only. Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh is licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0 (licenses/APACHE-2.0.txt). The following files are
redistributed unmodified.
