UtauTTS policy for JSUT-derived prosody models
===============================================

Scope
-----

This notice covers the following JSUT-derived files:

- `models/frame-intonation-v8.json`
- `models/prosody-multitask-v1.json`
- JSON files generated by `cmd/tools/jsut-target-prior`

Source data
-----------

- The two models are trained from JSUT audio
- Target priors contain statistics generated from JSUT audio and jsut-label
  data
- Source terms and attribution: `licenses/JSUT-DATA-AND-LABELS.txt`
- Policy basis: [official JSUT terms](https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut)

Distribution conditions
-----------------------

Permitted uses under this UtauTTS policy:

- academic research
- non-commercial research, including research within commercial organizations
- personal use

Commercial use of the models, target priors, or adaptations requires prior
permission from the JSUT rights holders. JSUT audio publication follows the
limited website or blog publication described in the official terms, such as
about 100 audio files.

Attribution
-----------

Redistributed models, target priors, and adaptations retain this notice,
`licenses/JSUT-DATA-AND-LABELS.txt`, and the source attribution recorded in the
model metadata. JSUT source data use and attribution follow the upstream
documents.

The UtauTTS source code uses the repository-level MIT License. Models, target
priors, voicebanks, and other third-party assets use the conditions recorded in
their respective notices.
