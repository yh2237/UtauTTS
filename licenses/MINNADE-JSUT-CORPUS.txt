みんなで作るJSUTコーパスbasic5000
==================================

Corpus: みんなで作るJSUTコーパスbasic5000, BASIC5000_0001-0600

Source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/minnade-jsut/

relative pitch (intonation) only and do not intentionally model the speaker's
voice quality. UtauTTS redistributes only the trained weights, not the corpus
