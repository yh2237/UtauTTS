LibriSpeech pronunciation lexicon
=================================

UtauTTS embeds a filtered subset of the LibriSpeech pronunciation lexicon for
English words that are not in the bundled CMUdict.

- Source: LibriSpeech language models, vocabulary and G2P models (OpenSLR SLR11)
  https://www.openslr.org/11/
- License: public domain
- Embedded file: internal/g2p/lexicon/librispeech.dict.gz
- Filtering: words already in CMUdict, non-alphabetic entries, one-letter
  entries, and entries without a vowel were removed. Duplicate words keep the
  first pronunciation.
- Entries: 133,709
- SHA-256:
  596EEA4E72C0D162EFD1695C6198EBC52786E8F1131F0BBC49EA266CA39F1764

The lexicon contains pronunciations for words in the LibriSpeech vocabulary,
some of which were generated automatically by the LibriSpeech preparation
scripts. CMUdict is always preferred when a word exists in both dictionaries.
