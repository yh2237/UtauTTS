Frame intonation v9.1T
======================

Model id: frame-intonation-tcn-v9.1-t

This model predicts frame-level relative pitch (intonation) for Japanese
text-to-speech. It learns only relative pitch and does not reproduce the voice
quality of its training speakers. Its weights are distributed under the MIT
License (see LICENSE).

Training corpora (both spoken by Rei Yumesaki):

1. Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1 "Voice Actress Statistics Corpus"
   (JVS-corpus compatible).
   Source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/corpus/

2. みんなで作るJSUTコーパスbasic5000, BASIC5000_0001-0600.
   Source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/minnade-jsut/

The terms of use for each corpus are published at the source URLs above. Their
wording governs; this notice does not replace or reinterpret it. UtauTTS does
not redistribute the corpus audio.
