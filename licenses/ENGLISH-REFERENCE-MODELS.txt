English reference models (models/english-reference)
====================================================

UtauTTS reads English sentences with Kokoro-82M, compares the speech with the
voicebank recordings through HuBERT Base features, and synthesizes the output
from the voicebank frames only. The following files are redistributed
unmodified. All of them are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0;
the license text is in licenses/APACHE-2.0.txt.
