English reference models (models/english-reference)
====================================================

UtauTTS reads English and Spanish sentences with Kokoro-82M, compares the speech with the
voicebank recordings through WavLM features, and synthesizes the output
from the voicebank frames only. The following files are redistributed
unmodified. Kokoro-82M, the misaki lexicon, and HuBERT Base are licensed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0 (licenses/APACHE-2.0.txt). WavLM is licensed
under the MIT License (licenses/WAVLM-MIT.txt).
