JSUT and jsut-label provenance
=============================

Scope
-----

This notice records the upstream data for the JSUT-derived model files
`frame-intonation-v8` and `prosody-multitask-v1`. UtauTTS release contents are
the model JSON files and license notices. Source data locations: the upstream
JSUT and jsut-label repositories.

JSUT corpus
-----------

JSUT is the Japanese speech corpus of the Saruwatari-lab., University of
Tokyo. The [official JSUT page](https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut)
defines separate terms for text and audio:

- Text: CC-BY-SA 4.0 and other source terms
- Audio use: academic research, non-commercial research including research
  within commercial organizations, and personal use
- Audio publication: the limited website or blog publication described on the
  official page, such as about 100 audio files
- Commercial use: contact the JSUT rights holders listed on the official page

The BASIC5000 text sources and their conditions are listed in the upstream
licence information:

- Wikipedia text: CC-BY-SA 3.0
  https://ja.wikipedia.org/
- TANAKA corpus text: CC-BY 2.0
  http://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/Tanaka_Corpus
- JSUT text: CC-BY-SA 4.0
  https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut

Reference:
Ryosuke Sonobe, Shinnosuke Takamichi, and Hiroshi Saruwatari,
"JSUT corpus: free large-scale Japanese speech corpus for end-to-end speech
synthesis," arXiv preprint 1711.00354, 2017.

jsut-label BASIC5000 labels
---------------------------

The training tools use the BASIC5000 HTS-style labels from:
https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label

The upstream README describes:

- manually annotated phonetic and prosodic information
- time information estimated by Julius
- Tomoki Koriyama as the main contributor and Shinnosuke Takamichi as a
  contributor
- label data under CC-BY-SA 4.0 and other source terms

The upstream [LICENCE.txt](https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label/blob/master/LICENCE.txt)
lists the component text conditions. Attribution and share-alike conditions
for jsut-label data and derivatives follow those upstream documents.

Model-specific provenance
-------------------------

- `frame-intonation-v8`: Open JTalk provides accent and linguistic features
- A candidate trained with `--jsut-context-labels`: jsut-label provides accent
  contexts
- Model distribution policy: `licenses/PROSODY-MODELS.txt`
