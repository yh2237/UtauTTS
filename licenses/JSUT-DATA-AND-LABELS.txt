JSUT and jsut-label provenance
==============================

The official bundled learned prosody models were trained from the JSUT Japanese
speech corpus and are distributed with this application as model files. UtauTTS
also bundles a selection of 300 unchanged BASIC5000 sentences as prompts for
manual prosody adjustment. The original JSUT recordings and the complete label
repository are not bundled in UtauTTS.

JSUT corpus
-----------

JSUT (Japanese speech corpus of Saruwatari-lab., University of Tokyo) is used as
the training corpus for the learned prosody models. The JSUT distribution
identifies the component sources and licenses of its text data in its LICENCE
file. BASIC5000 includes text from the sources listed below.

The JSUT official terms distinguish the text from the audio. The audio may be
used for academic research, non-commercial research (including research within
commercial organizations), and personal use. The audio may not be redistributed.
Commercial use requires contacting the JSUT rights holders. This notice does
not redistribute JSUT audio and does not grant commercial rights to the bundled
prosody models; see the official JSUT page for the current terms and contacts:
https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut

Upstream information:
https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Reference:
Ryosuke Sonobe, Shinnosuke Takamichi, and Hiroshi Saruwatari,
"JSUT corpus: free large-scale Japanese speech corpus for end-to-end speech
synthesis," arXiv preprint 1711.00354, 2017.

Bundled BASIC5000 prompt selection
----------------------------------

The GUI resource qt/prosody-prompts-ja-v1.json contains 300 sentences selected
from JSUT BASIC5000. The sentence text is unchanged. UtauTTS assigns the selected
sentences to six 50-sentence packs and adds its own separate 10-sentence starter
pack. Selection metadata records the source transcript SHA-256 and tool used.

The applicable terms identified by the upstream JSUT LICENCE file are:

- TANAKA corpus text: CC BY 2.0
  http://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/Tanaka_Corpus
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/
- Japanese Wikipedia text: CC BY-SA 3.0
  https://ja.wikipedia.org/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- JSUT original sentences: CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Upstream JSUT information and licence mapping:
https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut

When redistributing the bundled prompt resource or exported teacher data that
contains these sentences, preserve this attribution and the applicable Creative
Commons terms. No JSUT audio is included in the prompt resource.

jsut-label BASIC5000 labels
---------------------------

The optional BASIC5000 HTS-style labels are obtained from:
https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label

The repository credits Tomoki Koriyama as the main contributor and Shinnosuke
Takamichi as a contributor. The label data is described there as CC BY-SA 4.0,
with component text data carrying the following source terms:

- Wikipedia text: CC BY-SA 3.0
  https://ja.wikipedia.org/
- TANAKA corpus text: CC BY 2.0
  http://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/Tanaka_Corpus
- JSUT text: CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut

The labels also contain manual phonetic/prosodic annotation and time estimates
made using Julius. Derived label data must preserve the applicable attribution
and share-alike terms. See the upstream repository and its LICENCE.txt for the
complete terms.

Model-specific provenance
-------------------------

The self-describing model JSON files for the official JSUT-derived models
identify their training provenance and point back to this notice. This notice
is included in the release license bundle even though the original corpus and
label files are not included.
