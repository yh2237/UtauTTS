UtauTTS uses selected AISHELL-3 utterances to train bounded relative-pitch
corrections to Mandarin tone curves. The model does not intentionally model
the speakers' voice quality. Preprocessing aligns syllables and extracts
normalized pitch contours. Corpus audio, transcripts, and alignment files
are not redistributed.
