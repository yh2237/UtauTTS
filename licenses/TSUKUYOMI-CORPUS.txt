Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1
===========================

Corpus: Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1 "Voice Actress Statistics Corpus"
        (JVS-corpus compatible), spoken by Rei Yumesaki.
Source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/corpus/

UtauTTS uses this corpus to train frame-intonation models. Those models learn
only relative pitch (intonation) from the corpus and do not reproduce the
speaker's voice quality. UtauTTS does not redistribute the corpus audio. The
model weights are distributed under the MIT License (see LICENSE).
