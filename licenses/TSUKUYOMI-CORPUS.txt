Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1
===========================

Corpus: つくよみちゃんコーパス Vol.1 声優統計コーパス（JVSコーパス準拠）
        (Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1, a "voice-actress statistics" corpus,
        JVS compatible), spoken by 夢前黎 / Rei Yumesaki.
Source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/corpus/

UtauTTS uses this corpus to train frame-intonation models. Those models learn
relative pitch (intonation) only and do not intentionally model the speaker's
voice quality. UtauTTS redistributes only the trained weights, not the corpus
audio or scripts. The model weights are distributed under the MIT License
(see LICENSE).
