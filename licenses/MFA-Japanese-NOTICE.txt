Used locally to estimate phoneme boundaries for the frame-intonation-tcn-v10
training corpora (tools/align-intonation-mfa.py). The acoustic model weights
are not bundled with the UtauTTS intonation model. Alignment results are
converted into UtauTTS mora intervals.
