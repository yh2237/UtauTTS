UtauTTS uses selected AISHELL-3 utterances to train the timing of phoneme
transitions only. Only normalized envelope movement is used to retime the
voicebank output; no corpus audio or predicted envelope is rendered, and the
model does not intentionally model the speakers' voice quality.
Preprocessing aligns initials and finals and extracts normalized envelope
movement. Corpus audio, transcripts, and alignment files are not
redistributed.
