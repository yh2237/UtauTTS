Irodori-TTS teacher data for intonation-ja-v11
==============================================

The F0 head of intonation-ja-v11 was trained on relative pitch measured from
speech that UtauTTS generated locally with a third-party text-to-speech model.
UtauTTS redistributes only the trained F0 head weights (inside the model JSON),
not the generated audio, the scripts, or the text-to-speech model.

Text-to-speech model (teacher)
    Irodori-TTS v4.1-Small, Aratako
    https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-v4.1-Small
    https://github.com/Aratako/Irodori-TTS
    License: MIT License
Audio codec used by the teacher
    Semantic-DACVAE-Japanese-32dim, Aratako
    https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Semantic-DACVAE-Japanese-32dim
    License: MIT License
Speaker reference given to the teacher
    Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1 (CV. 夢前黎), VOICEACTRESS100_031-038
    (see licenses/TSUKUYOMI-CORPUS.txt)
Scripts read by the teacher
    - BASIC5000 sentences from jsut-label (text_level0), with readings used to
      discard sentences whose Open JTalk reading differed.
      https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label
      The jsut-label text licence lists Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 3.0), the Tanaka
      Corpus (CC BY 2.0) and JSUT (CC BY-SA 4.0) as the sources of the text.
    - Everyday sentences written for UtauTTS.
Alignment
    Montreal Forced Aligner, japanese_mfa (see licenses/MFA-Japanese-NOTICE.txt)

The model learns relative pitch (intonation) only and does not intentionally
reproduce any speaker's voice. The publishers' terms at the URLs above govern
the use of their models and texts and remain the authority; this notice is
informational and does not restate or interpret them.
