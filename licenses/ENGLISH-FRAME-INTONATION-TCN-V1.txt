UtauTTS uses selected LibriTTS-R utterances to train relative-pitch contours
only. The model does not intentionally model the speakers' voice quality.
Preprocessing selects utterances, excludes reported restoration failures,
aligns phonemes, and extracts normalized pitch contours. Corpus audio,
scripts, and MFA alignment model weights are not redistributed.
