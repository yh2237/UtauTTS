UtauTTS English Speech Timing Target v1
=======================================

Model: speech-timing-target-en-v1 (embedded in the WORLD bridge)

UtauTTS uses selected LibriTTS-R utterances to train the timing of phoneme
transitions only. Only normalized envelope movement is used to retime the
voicebank output; no corpus audio or predicted envelope is rendered, and the
model does not intentionally model the speakers' voice quality. Preprocessing
selects utterances, aligns phonemes, and extracts normalized envelope
movement. Corpus audio, scripts, and MFA alignment model weights are not
redistributed.
