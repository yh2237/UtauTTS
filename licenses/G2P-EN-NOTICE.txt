English OOV pronunciation model (g2p-en)
=========================================

UtauTTS embeds the out-of-vocabulary grapheme-to-phoneme model of g2p-en for
English words that are not in the bundled CMUdict or LibriSpeech lexicon. The model is a two-layer GRU
encoder-decoder. Only the trained weights are converted to float32 and embedded
as internal/g2p/en/english-g2p.bin; the Python implementation is not bundled.

- Original project: g2p-en by Kyubyong Park and Jongseok Kim
  https://github.com/Kyubyong/g2p
- Weights and conversion: g2p-en-plus 2.0.3 (fork maintained by Myron)
  https://pypi.org/project/g2p-en-plus/
- Model file: checkpoint20.npz
