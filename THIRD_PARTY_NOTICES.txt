JSUT-derived prosody models and jsut-label
==========================================

This section covers the bundled JSUT-derived models
models/frame-intonation-v8.json and models/prosody-multitask-v1.json, together
with JSON target priors generated by cmd/tools/jsut-target-prior. The models
are trained from JSUT audio. The training tools use the BASIC5000 HTS-style
labels from https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label. Source terms and
attribution are recorded in licenses/JSUT-DATA-AND-LABELS.txt.

The [official JSUT terms](https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/publication/jsut)
define separate text and audio conditions:

- Text: CC-BY-SA 4.0 and other source terms
- Audio use: academic research, non-commercial research including research
  within commercial organizations, and personal use
- Audio publication: the limited website or blog publication described on the
  official page, such as about 100 audio files
- Commercial use: contact the JSUT rights holders listed on the official page

Model and target-prior distribution policy: the academic research,
non-commercial research, and personal-use terms in
licenses/PROSODY-MODELS.txt. Commercial use requires prior permission from the
JSUT rights holders.

The english-intonation-v1 model contains original UtauTTS coefficients and is
distributed under the MIT License. Its notice is
licenses/ENGLISH-INTONATION-V1.txt. UtauTTS source code uses the repository MIT
License; models, prompts, voicebanks, and other third-party assets use their
respective notices.


DiffSinger ONNX Runtime and DirectML bridge
===========================================

The Full Windows package includes the optional DiffSinger bridge. It embeds
ONNX Runtime with the DirectML execution provider and the Microsoft DirectML
runtime. The Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.DirectML and
Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.Managed packages have identical license and
third-party notice files. Those files are included once as
ONNXRUNTIME-LICENSE.txt and ONNXRUNTIME-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt. The three
Microsoft.AI.DirectML notices remain under runtime/licenses alongside the .NET
runtime notices. Package profiles: Full Windows = bridge included; Japanese
Windows, Linux, and macOS = bridge absent.

Upstream package pages:
https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.DirectML/1.23.0
https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.Managed/1.23.0
https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.AI.DirectML/1.15.4
