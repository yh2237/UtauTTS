audiodsp
========

MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 yh. It has no external module dependencies.
Its license text is identical to utauio's and is included once as
licenses/Go/github_com_yh2237_utauio-v0.4.0-LICENSE.txt.

Source: https://github.com/yh2237/audiodsp


gograd
======

CPU, CUDA, DirectML and Vulkan inference, PyTorch checkpoint reading,
PyTorch-compatible random numbers and resampling, and in its training tools
for automatic differentiation and CUDA training. The library is distributed
under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 yh. Its license text is identical to
utauio's and is included once as
licenses/Go/github_com_yh2237_utauio-v0.4.0-LICENSE.txt. On Linux, its Vulkan
calls use github.com/ebitengine/purego (see below).

English OOV pronunciation model (g2p-en)
=========================================

UtauTTS embeds the out-of-vocabulary grapheme-to-phoneme GRU weights of g2p-en
for English words that are not in CMUdict or LibriSpeech. Only the trained weights are
converted to float32 and embedded as internal/g2p/en/english-g2p.bin. The
model and its conversion are from g2p-en-plus 2.0.3 (Apache License 2.0), based
on g2p-en by Kyubyong Park and Jongseok Kim. See licenses/G2P-EN-NOTICE.txt.

Upstream source:
https://github.com/Kyubyong/g2p
https://pypi.org/project/g2p-en-plus/


Bundled prosody models
======================

The bundled Japanese frame-intonation models are UtauTTS weights distributed under the
MIT License. They were trained on third-party corpora, and UtauTTS does not
bundle or redistribute the corpus audio or scripts. The applicable notices are
included in the package under licenses/ (model-to-notice mapping:
models/README.md): licenses/TSUKUYOMI-CORPUS.txt and
licenses/MINNADE-JSUT-CORPUS.txt. The training data of the base model
(frame-intonation-tcn-v10, embedded in frame-intonation-tcn-v12) was aligned locally with the Montreal Forced Aligner Japanese MFA acoustic
model (CC BY 4.0), which is not bundled; see licenses/MFA-Japanese-NOTICE.txt.

The F0 head of frame-intonation-tcn-v12 (the default Japanese model) was also
trained on relative pitch measured from speech that UtauTTS generated locally
with Irodori-TTS v4.1-Small (MIT License); the generated audio, scripts and
the text-to-speech model are not bundled. See
licenses/IRODORI-TEACHER-NOTICE.txt.

The speech-timing-target-v1 weights (internal/speechtiming, embedded in the
UtauTTS binaries) are UtauTTS weights distributed under the MIT License. They were
trained on the same Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol.1 and Minnade JSUT basic5000
BASIC5000_0001-0600 data as the base model of frame-intonation-tcn-v12 with the same notices
(licenses/TSUKUYOMI-CORPUS.txt, licenses/MINNADE-JSUT-CORPUS.txt and
licenses/MFA-Japanese-NOTICE.txt). Only normalized envelope movement is used to
retime the voicebank output; no corpus audio or predicted envelope is rendered.

Reference matching uses Kokoro-82M, Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh and the misaki
English lexicon from models/reference, redistributed unmodified under the Apache License 2.0, and
WavLM base+ (microsoft/wavlm-base-plus), redistributed under the MIT License;
see licenses/REFERENCE-MODELS.txt, licenses/CHINESE-REFERENCE-MODEL.txt and
licenses/WAVLM-MIT.txt.

UtauTTS source code uses the repository MIT License; models, prompts,
voicebanks, and other third-party assets use their respective notices.
